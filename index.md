A flat tyre always seems to happen at the worst time. It might be on a hot afternoon, late at night or on a busy road with cars passing close by. The good news is that a flat tyre is one of the easiest breakdowns to handle if you stay calm and know your options.

Before anything else, think about where you are. If the road is busy or you have no safe place to stop, it is better to call a [recovery near me](https://www.recovery-near.com/) service than to risk changing the wheel next to fast traffic. Your safety is worth more than the time you save.

**First, slow down gently.** Do not brake hard. Hold the steering wheel firmly, turn on your hazard lights and move to the shoulder or the nearest safe spot.

**Check the damage.** A small puncture is one thing. A torn sidewall or a bent rim is another. If the wheel looks damaged, do not drive on it, even for a short distance.

**Change it yourself only if it is safe.** You need a spare that has air in it, a jack and a wheel spanner. The ground must be flat and firm. If you are on a slope, soft sand or a narrow road, wait for help instead.

**Call for help when:**
- You have no spare, or the spare is also flat
- The wheel nuts are stuck
- You are on a highway or busy road
- The rim is damaged
- It is very hot or very late
